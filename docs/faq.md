# FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes. In return, your recorder also runs some live checks for other users, from
your IP address. [What the recorder sends to VodLabs](data.md#shared-checks-what-the-free-plan-asks-in-return)
explains what that means.

**Why does it need the VodLabs service?**
Knowing the moment someone goes live means asking each platform over and
over. The service does that for everyone at once and tells your recorder when
a streamer you follow goes live, so your machine isn't polling every platform
itself. The recording itself is done by your machine.

**Does VodLabs see my recordings?**
No. Video goes straight from the platform to your disk, and stays there.

**Can I use it without the service?**
Not for recording new streams: without the service the recorder can't tell
when anyone goes live. Recordings that are already running carry on if the
service goes away, and everything you've recorded stays yours.

**Does it record while my computer is off?**
No. The recorder runs on your computer, so it records only while that is on
and the app is running.

**How much disk space do recordings use?**
It depends on the stream. A 1080p stream is typically 1 to 3 GB per hour.
The app warns when a recording folder is running low.

**Does it record chat?**
No, only the video and audio.

**Can I run it on more than one computer?**
Yes. Each installation is independent: it has its own streamers, recordings
and settings.

**Can you add a platform?**
Open a [feature request](https://github.com/vodlabs-io/recorder/issues/new/choose).
Platform support is built into the VodLabs service as well as the app, so new
platforms come from VodLabs rather than from pull requests.

**Is it open source?**
It is source-available: you can read, build and change the code, and share
your changes, for noncommercial use. [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) explains
the license and how to get a commercial one.

**Can I use my recordings however I like?**
The recordings are other people's broadcasts. Respect the creators' rights and
each platform's terms when you keep or share them.
