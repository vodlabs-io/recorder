# Licensing

VodLabs Recorder is source-available under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). This page explains what that
means in practice. If it and the license ever disagree, the license is what
applies.

## What you can do

You may use, study, change and share the recorder for any noncommercial
purpose. That includes:

- recording streams to watch later, for yourself;
- personal study, research, hobby projects and experiments;
- building it from source, changing it, and sharing your changes;
- use by charities, schools, public research, public safety and health
  organizations, and government institutions.

When you share a copy, changed or not, include the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file (or
its URL) and the `Required Notice:` line at its top.

## What needs a commercial license

Anything with a commercial purpose, for example:

- running a monetized channel, page or account that posts recordings or clips
  made with the recorder;
- recording on behalf of clients, or selling recordings or access to them;
- bundling the recorder, or software built from it, into a paid product or
  service.

To ask about a commercial license, email dev@vodlabs.io.

## Parts under other licenses

Some parts of the recorder are other people's work and keep their own
licenses. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) lists them. The main
one is FFmpeg: the recorder embeds a separate FFmpeg program licensed under the
GNU GPL version 2 or later, whose source is published with each
[custom-ffmpeg](https://github.com/vodlabs-io/custom-ffmpeg) release.

## Names and logos

The license covers the code, not the VodLabs name or logo. If you distribute a
modified version, give it a different name and icon, so no one mistakes it for
the official app.
